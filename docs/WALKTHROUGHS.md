# Walkthroughs (non-technical)

## Corps member
1. **Sign in** → Dashboard shows your enrolled courses and progress.
2. **Courses**: browse newest first; use search, skill, type (In-Camp / Post-Camp), state and LGA filters; use *Next* for more.
3. **In-Camp SAED**: courses held at your orientation camp, with timetables. (Access ends after camp + grace period; Post-Camp courses stay available.)
4. Open a course → **Enrol now**. You are enrolled *and* connected to the trainer immediately.
5. Open lessons inside the app (video, PDF, text). Free lessons open at once. Click **Mark as complete** to track progress.
6. **Premium lesson?** You will see its price. Either **Pay with Paystack** (opens a secure window, unlocks automatically), or **pay the trainer by bank transfer** using the account shown and press *I have paid — notify trainer*. The trainer unlocks it after confirming.
7. If a payment says **"under review"**, you have not lost money — Dunis Admin will confirm it and you will be notified. If it says "still processing", wait; access appears automatically.
8. **My Enrollments** lists every course with a progress bar and an *Open course* button. **Trainers** lets you find trainers (every filter optional) and see their courses.

## Trainer
1. After activation payment (one-off), go to **My Courses → New course** (type, dates, location). Publish when ready.
2. Open the course → **Add lesson**. Upload a **document** (free) or add a **video** (a one-off ₦2,000 per-lesson fee may apply; the app asks you to confirm and pay first). Paste a YouTube/Vimeo link instead if you prefer (same fee rule).
3. Make a lesson premium: tick **Lock this lesson** and enter a price. Untick to make it free again. Use ↑/↓ to reorder.
4. **Access** on a locked lesson: grant free access, revoke access, or confirm a bank transfer (also shown as a banner at the top of the course).
5. **Corps Members** shows your students. **In-Camp Sessions** manages scheduled sessions.
6. **Earnings**: add and verify your payout account (Paystack confirms the account name). Paid lessons are split automatically; the page shows your share, the platform share and payments.

## SAED Admin
Dashboard overview; **Users** (approve/manage trainers and users); **Courses** (view, restrict/unrestrict, edit); **Enrollments** (existing review tools). Can view fee settings but not change them.

## Dunis Admin
**Payments & Fees**: (1) *Fees & settings* — set the video upload fee and switch it on/off, platform share %, activation fee, grace days; (2) *Payments* — filter every payment; for **needs review** items press Review → Approve or Reject with a note; (3) *Trainer activation* — re-verify a trainer's activation payment with Paystack; (4) *Lesson access* — who unlocked what and how.
